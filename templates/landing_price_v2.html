Choose the invoice currency and exchange rate once for the full shipment. All product lines use the same settings.

Amount is quantity × per-unit price, shown in INR. For USD, the calculator converts using the live interbank USD/INR rate plus 2%. INR prices need no exchange rate.

Choose a BOM to import MPN, unit price, and quantity automatically. Excel, CSV, TSV, TXT, and text-based PDF files are supported; scanned image PDFs need OCR and should be exported to CSV or Excel. 2. Assessable value & charges

Insurance is 1.125% of invoice value. When total shipment invoice is below ₹7,000, each line’s freight is (line invoice ÷ total invoice) × ₹1,200. At or above ₹7,000, freight is zero. Assessable value is invoice + insurance + allocated freight; other charges are excluded from BCD assessment.

4. Shipment charges

Freight is (line invoice ÷ total invoice) × ₹1,200 only when total shipment invoice is below ₹7,000. Enabled remittance, CHA, domestic trucking, and other charges are allocated by INR invoice share. Other charges are entered in the assessable-value section.

Margin is applied to each line’s landed cost. Landing price per unit includes the margin and updates when you recalculate; at 0% margin it equals the landed cost per unit. IGST is optional and, when enabled, is calculated after margin.
